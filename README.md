# 🍲 Food Hunter – Food Donation and Management System

## 📌 About the Project

**Food Hunter** is a digital platform designed to connect NGOs that need food assistance with donors who are willing to donate food. The main aim of this project is to reduce food shortages, improve food donation coordination, and support hunger relief efforts.

Through this platform, NGOs can submit food requests, and donors can view those requests and contribute according to their availability. The system aims to make food donation and distribution more organized, efficient, and reliable.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Connect NGOs with food donors through a digital platform.
- Help people in need receive food assistance.
- Reduce food wastage and food shortages.
- Improve coordination between donors and NGOs.
- Support safe and timely food delivery.
- Make food donation management easier and more efficient.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Food Requests:** NGOs can request food assistance through the platform.
- **Donor Contributions:** Donors can view food requests and contribute accordingly.
- **Real-Time Tracking:** Track food delivery progress.
- **Notifications:** Keep users informed about requests and delivery updates.
- **Food Quality Checks:** Support food safety and quality verification.
- **Efficient Coordination:** Improve communication between donors and NGOs.
- **Hunger Relief Support:** Help provide food to people who need it.

## 👥 Users

### NGOs
- Submit food requests.
- View request status.
- Coordinate food assistance and delivery.

### Donors
- View available food requests.
- Select requests to support.
- Contribute food to help people in need.

## 🌍 Project Impact

Food Hunter aims to create a more connected and efficient food donation system. By bringing NGOs and donors together, the platform can help improve food distribution, reduce wastage, and support communities facing food shortages.
